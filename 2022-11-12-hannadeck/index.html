hannadeck

November 2022

I wanted to make a tarot-like deck of cards for my friend Hanna. After trying a few methods, I settled on making goopy sharpie illustrations and rendering them as stencils to be printed with my ender. Each card represents something about Hanna. 

Each card started as a quick brush pen illustration on paper, then was photographed, traced to SVG, extruded in Fusion 360, and printed flat on an FDM printer. The illustrations become cut-through stencils — hold a card up to the light and the drawing glows through. 36 cards in total, each one a different character. Assets below are grouped by folder on disk (HEIC masters live in heic/ and are omitted here).
