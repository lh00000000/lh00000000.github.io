Hanna Deck

November 2022

A deck of 36 stencil cards, each featuring a hand-drawn illustration of a person defined by a single quirky activity. Drawn with brush pen on paper, vectorized into SVGs, extruded into 3D card models, and 3D printed as thin black stencil cards you can hold up to the light.

Drawing

Each card started as a quick brush pen illustration on paper. The constraint: one figure, one activity, captured in a few bold strokes that would read clearly as a cut-out silhouette.

Vectorizing

The pen drawings were photographed and traced into black-and-white SVGs, cleaning up the shapes while keeping the hand-drawn character. Each illustration was placed inside a card layout with a title at the top and bottom.

3D Printing

The SVG outlines were extruded into thin 3D card bodies in Fusion 360, then printed flat on an FDM printer. The illustrations become cut-through stencils — hold a card up to the light and the drawing glows through.

The Full Deck

36 cards in total, each one a different character.
